# Amos Khan - Personal Portfolio

**Live site:** [amosportfolio.vercel.app](https://amosportfolio.vercel.app/)

## Welcome to My Portfolio

Hello! I'm Amos Khan, a **Sports Science & Management graduate** from Nanyang Technological University (NTU), based in Singapore. I'm currently training with the **Ministry of Education (MOE)** to become a **Physical Education teacher**, through the Postgraduate Diploma in Education (PE) at the National Institute of Education (NIE).

My passions span education, sports science research and building with technology, from exercise physiology studies to AI tools such as [Kinetix Lab](https://kinetix-lab.vercel.app/) and the [SG PE Syllabus Bot](https://sg-pe-syllabus-bot.vercel.app/) that help PE teachers analyse movement and navigate the syllabus.

## Portfolio Highlights

*   **Projects**: AI tools for PE, final-year exercise physiology research, photography and badminton coaching.
*   **Resume**: Work experience, education and skills at a glance, with a downloadable resume.
*   **Updates**: Reflections and milestones from internships, research and teaching.
*   **Responsive & Accessible**: Works across devices, supports light and dark mode, keyboard navigation and reduced motion.

## Technologies Utilized

*   [React](https://reactjs.org/)
*   [Next.js](https://nextjs.org/)
*   [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/)
*   [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/)
*   [Framer Motion](https://www.framer.com/motion/) & [Headless UI](https://headlessui.com/)

## Editing Content

All content lives in `src/data/sections/`, one file per section (`hero.tsx`, `about.ts`, `portfolio.ts`, `resume.tsx`, `skills.ts`, `blog.ts`, `testimonials.ts`, `contact.ts`). Page metadata such as the title, description and social preview image is set in `src/data/data.tsx`.
